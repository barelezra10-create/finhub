An airline card should fit routes you can actually book. A mileage balance has limited practical value if the seats you need are unavailable or the cash surcharges erase the savings.

## Check your usual trips first

Look up award availability on realistic dates before valuing a welcome offer. Read baggage-benefit rules for the cardholder and companions, booking requirements and eligible flights. Check whether points transfers are reversible before moving rewards.

## Example: baggage benefits and the annual fee

If a hypothetical card saves $35 per eligible checked bag in each direction, two qualifying round trips save $140. Against a $95 annual fee, that leaves $45 before other benefits or costs. If you normally travel with carry-on luggage, those hypothetical bag savings are zero.

## Do not confuse earning rate with value

Multiply miles earned by the value of your intended redemption, after required cash charges. An apparently higher miles rate does not establish a better return. Compare [general travel cards](/credit-cards/travel) and the [travel valuation example](/best/travel) before committing to one program.

## How to use this guide

The calculations use hypothetical terms to show the trade-offs; they do not describe a live offer. Check the official issuer disclosures linked from each card profile for reward eligibility, fees and redemption conditions.
