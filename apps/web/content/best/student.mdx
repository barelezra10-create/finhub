Student status alone does not establish card eligibility. Start with application requirements and affordability, then compare rewards on the purchases already in your budget.

## Understand the under-21 requirements

For applicants under 21, issuers generally need evidence of independent ability to pay or a qualifying co-signer where offered. Do not assume family support or student status automatically satisfies an application. Follow the issuer’s instructions for reporting income.

## Example: rewards at a student budget

On a hypothetical $300 in eligible monthly purchases, 1.5% cash back returns $54 per year. A 2% rate returns $72: an $18 difference before fees or interest. A small rewards improvement may be less important than avoiding an annual fee or choosing an account you can manage consistently.

## Plan for life after graduation

Ask the issuer what happens to the account, fees and benefits when your student status changes. An automatic upgrade is not something to assume. Compare [first-card basics](/best/first-time) and [no-fee cards](/credit-cards/no-fee) before applying.
