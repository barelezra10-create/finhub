Choose a travel card around trips you expect to take and benefits you would otherwise pay for. A large advertised package of credits is not the same as money saved.

## Build a realistic benefits budget

Check eligible booking channels, credit expiration, enrollment requirements, lounge guest rules and foreign transaction fees. Assign no value to a benefit you do not expect to use. Confirm redemption terms for your actual itinerary instead of assuming every point has a fixed value.

## Example: calculate redemption value

If the same hypothetical itinerary costs $300 in cash or 25,000 points plus $50, the points replace $250 of cash: 1 cent per point. Compare equivalent cancellation and baggage terms. Then subtract the card’s annual fee when estimating the total yearly value.

## Choose flexibility or a specific program

A general travel program and an airline or hotel card solve different needs. Read the [miles guide](/best/miles) and [hotel guide](/best/hotel), then compare the result with [cash back](/best/cashback). Do not count a first-year bonus as a recurring annual benefit.

## How to use this guide

The calculations use hypothetical terms to show the trade-offs; they do not describe a live offer. Check the official issuer disclosures linked from each card profile for reward eligibility, fees and redemption conditions.
