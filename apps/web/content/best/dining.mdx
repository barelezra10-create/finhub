A dining card works best when eligible restaurant spending is already a meaningful part of your budget. Check which transactions count before applying the headline rate to every food purchase.

## Restaurants, delivery and grocery purchases

Read the issuer’s category definitions for delivery services, bars, bakeries and restaurants inside other businesses. Compare grocery spending separately. A transaction’s rewards treatment can depend on its merchant classification and the program terms.

## Example: price the extra dining reward

At $400 per month, annual dining spending is $4,800. A hypothetical 3% dining rate earns $144 versus $96 at 2%, a $48 improvement. A $95 annual fee would exceed that improvement unless other usable benefits make up the difference. No bonuses or interest are included.

## Cash back or points?

For points, use the redemption you intend to make and subtract any associated costs. Count dining credits only where they replace purchases you would otherwise make. Compare [grocery strategies](/best/groceries) and [cash back cards](/credit-cards/cash-back) for the rest of your food budget.

## How to use this guide

The calculations use hypothetical terms to show the trade-offs; they do not describe a live offer. Check the official issuer disclosures linked from each card profile for reward eligibility, fees and redemption conditions.
