Your first card should be easy to manage on your existing budget. A complicated bonus is less useful than clear fees, payment reminders and a limit you can comfortably repay.

## Student or secured?

If eligible, compare student accounts with standard starter offers. If a deposit is required, make sure it will not consume money needed for bills. Read the [student guide](/best/student) and [secured-card guide](/best/secured) for the different questions each application raises.

## Example: start with a small routine

With a hypothetical $500 limit and a $40 recurring purchase, that purchase represents 8% of the limit before any other balance. This illustrates utilization, not an ideal score target or a guarantee. Budget for the full statement and monitor other charges, fees and the payment due date.

## Before submitting an application

Confirm income requirements, ongoing fees, purchase APR and the issuer’s credit reporting. Set alerts and check that automatic payments have enough funds. Our [grace-period glossary](/glossary/grace-period) explains the term you will see in the account disclosure.
