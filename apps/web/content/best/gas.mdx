Choose a gas card using both your fuel spending and the stations you actually visit. A strong reward at an inconvenient or more expensive station can leave you paying more overall.

## Percentage rewards versus cents per gallon

A cents-per-gallon discount changes as a percentage when pump prices move. Check eligible locations, payment requirements and any spending cap. For a general rewards card, verify treatment of warehouse-club fuel and purchases inside a station.

## Example: translate the discount

At a hypothetical $4 per gallon, 10 cents off is a 2.5% discount. A 3% reward on that same $4 price is 12 cents per gallon before fees and redemption conditions. Compare the actual posted prices too: the cheaper station may win even without a card discount.

## Include your annual mileage budget

At $200 a month in eligible fuel purchases, each extra percentage point is worth $24 annually. That puts an annual fee in perspective. Compare [flat-rate cash back](/best/cashback) and [no-fee cards](/credit-cards/no-fee) if fuel is only a small part of your spending.

## How to use this guide

The calculations use hypothetical terms to show the trade-offs; they do not describe a live offer. Check the official issuer disclosures linked from each card profile for reward eligibility, fees and redemption conditions.
