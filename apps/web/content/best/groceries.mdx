The best grocery rewards structure for your household depends on where you shop and how much spending qualifies. Do not apply a supermarket rate to your full food budget without checking exclusions.

## Map your stores to the reward terms

Check supermarkets, warehouse clubs, superstores, online grocery orders and delivery services separately. Look for calendar-year or account-year caps and the rate after the cap. A combined cap shared with another category leaves less room for grocery purchases.

## Example: calculate both sides of a cap

For a hypothetical 4% rate on the first $6,000 of eligible groceries and 1% thereafter, $8,000 in yearly purchases earns $260 before fees. Subtract a $95 annual fee and the result is $165. A no-fee 2% card returns $160 on the same spending: only $5 less. This excludes bonuses and interest.

## Use your last few statements

Split purchases by store rather than guessing at one grocery total. Compare [dining rewards](/best/dining) for restaurant spending and use [card comparison](/credit-cards/compare) to inspect the source-linked facts available for each product.

## How to use this guide

The calculations use hypothetical terms to show the trade-offs; they do not describe a live offer. Check the official issuer disclosures linked from each card profile for reward eligibility, fees and redemption conditions.
